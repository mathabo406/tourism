Our Beautiful Country

Lesotho is a Mountainous Country in Southern Africa. It id populaly known as the Kingdom in the Sky because of its high mountains and breathtaking landscapes. The country is famous for its beautiful scenery, traditional villages, fresh mountain air and friendly people. Its capital city is Maseru.

Culture and Traditions

Basotho culture is known for its traditional blankets, Basotho hats called Mokorotlo, music and customs.

Why Visit Lesotho

Lesotho is a wonderful destination for people who enjoy nature, adventure and cultural experiences. Visotors can enjoy mountain views, hiking, horse riding, traditional food and learning about the history of the Basotho people.
