Sehlabathebe National Park

Lesotho's oldest nature reserve, located in the Qacha's Nek District in the southeastern Maloti Mountains. the name translates to "plateau of the shield" in Sesotho describing the unique rolling grasslands and dramatic rock shields.

Experience the raw beauty of Sehlabathebe without worrying about where to sleep! Comfortable lodging self-catering options and campsites are ready for travelers looking to disconnect.Whether you want to fish the Tsoelikana River or hike under the flight of the bearded vulture, stay close to nature in the heart of Lesotho

Maletsunyane Falls

Maletsunyane falls is a famous waterfall near Semonkong in Lesotho. It is known for its impressive height and beautiful natural surroundings.

Looking for a base for your next Lesotho adventure? Rooms and chalets are ready for bookings at Maletsunyane Lodge.

Katse Dam

Katse Dam is a concrete double-curvature arch dam on the Malibamatso River in the Maluti Mountains of Lesotho.It is a centerpiece of the Lesotho Highlands Water Project, which diverts water to South Africa's industrial Gauteng province.

Looking for a breathtaking mountain getaway? Accommodation is open and ready for visitors at Katse Lodge.
